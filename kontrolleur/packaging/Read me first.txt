KONTROLLEUR
===========

Install: drag Kontrolleur onto the Applications folder next to it.
Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

FIRST START
Kontrolleur isn't from the App Store and isn't signed with an Apple developer
account, so macOS blocks it the first time ("Apple could not verify …").
Open it once anyway:

  1. Double-click Kontrolleur in Applications and close the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to
     "Kontrolleur was blocked …" and click "Open Anyway".
  3. Confirm with "Open Anyway" and your password.

After that it opens normally.
(Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Kontrolleur.app)

WHAT IT DOES
- 16n faderbank, Faderfox EC4 and DOREMiDi MPC-20 plugged into this Mac:
  choose "Cable" for the device and load / edit / upload its settings.
- DualSense, Pi functions and ER-301 routing: need a Raspberry Pi with
  github.com/ahabzutun/dualsense-eurorack-midi, reached over SSH
  (set the host under "SSH").

Settings live in ~/Library/Preferences/com.kontrolleur.Kontrolleur.plist,
notes in ~/Library/Application Support/Kontrolleur.
